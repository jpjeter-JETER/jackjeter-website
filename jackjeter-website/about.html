About Jack

Singer, songwriter, producer, and recording engineer based in Los Angeles.

Bio

I am a Los Angeles artist who writes, sings, and produces my own music. I released my first EP, The Red Place, in 2022. It has five electronic, house, and dance tracks. Since then I have been putting out singles, including LA Can’t Handle the Rain, in the past til i pass, ALL GO BAD, and my newest release, feelin' low (demo).

Most of what I know about making records I learned behind the board. I worked many sessions as an audio engineer, and some as a producer, at Kush Factory Studios on Melrose, where most of the work was rap, trap, and R&B. Now I work at a Grammy-winning pop studio in Studio City. Working on other artists' records taught me how to run a session, get a good vocal take, and finish a mix, and I bring all of that back to my own songs.

I record and mix in Pro Tools and Logic Pro. Right now I am focused on releasing new music and playing more live shows, and I am always open to working with other artists.

Short Press Bio

Jack Jeter is a Los Angeles singer, songwriter, and producer who also works as a recording engineer. After engineering and producing sessions at Kush Factory Studios on Melrose, mostly rap, trap, and R&B, he now works at a Grammy-winning pop studio in Studio City. His own catalog started with the 2022 electronic EP The Red Place and continues with singles like LA Can’t Handle the Rain, ALL GO BAD, and the new feelin' low (demo). He writes and produces his own music and is currently booking live dates.
